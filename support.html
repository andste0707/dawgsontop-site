Questions, bugs, or a league that won't behave? Email us and include your display name and league name.

support@dawgsontop.app

Sign-in trouble

Sign-in codes are emailed from noreply@dawgsontop.app and expire after a few minutes. Check your spam folder, then request a new code from the sign-in screen.

Joining a league

Ask your commissioner for the 6-character invite code, open the League tab, and enter it under "Join a league."

Deleting your account

Open the League tab and tap "Delete my account." This removes your login and email. Your past picks stay in league history as "Deleted member." You can also email privacy@dawgsontop.app.
